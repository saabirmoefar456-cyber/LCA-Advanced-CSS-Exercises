About Me

I'm a frontend web development trainee at Life Choices Academy in Cape Town. During the day I work in financial accounting at the Western Cape Government, and in my spare time I'm learning to build things on the web — one exercise at a time.

I enjoy the problem-solving side of coding: figuring out why a layout breaks, getting a hover effect just right, or making a page work on both a phone and a desktop. I'm drawn to clean design and writing code that actually makes sense when you read it back later.

Outside of code I have a strong interest in digital business and entrepreneurship, and I'm always thinking about how the web can solve real problems for people.
