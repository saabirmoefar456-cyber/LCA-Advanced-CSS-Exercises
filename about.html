Meet the Founder

"Africa is not just where I'm from — it's my inspiration, my canvas, and my greatest pride. Every stitch in our clothing tells a story."

Amara Dlamini grew up in Khayelitsha, Cape Town, surrounded by vibrant colours, music, and the rich tapestry of South African culture. From a young age, she had a passion for fashion — turning scraps of fabric into statement pieces that her community admired.

After completing a Fashion Design diploma at CPUT (Cape Peninsula University of Technology), Amara launched Afrika Vibes from her parents' garage in 2020 with a sewing machine, a WhatsApp group, and a big dream. Today, the brand ships to customers across South Africa and the African continent.

Her mission is simple: to make African culture wearable, accessible, and celebrated — while creating sustainable employment for local artisans and makers in Cape Town's townships.

Our Journey

2020

Afrika Vibes launched from a garage in Khayelitsha with 3 products and a WhatsApp group of 50 friends.

2021

First market stall at the Cape Town Neighbourgoods Market. Sold out in 4 hours! The brand started getting noticed.

2022

Launched our first e-commerce website. Expanded to 20+ products and started shipping nationally across South Africa.

2023

Partnered with 8 local artisans. Introduced the Kids Range and corporate bulk order programme.

2024

Named Best Emerging Lifestyle Brand at Cape Town Fashion Week. Began shipping to Botswana, Namibia & Zimbabwe.

2025 →

Launching our flagship store in Long Street, Cape Town. Watch this space! 🚀
